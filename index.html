Welcome

Windhoek on Jean is a small, owner-run guesthouse in Doringkloof, Centurion. Every one of our six rooms is set up the same way, so there's no guesswork — just a comfortable double room, your own bathroom, DSTV, and a proper breakfast to start the day. We keep bookings personal: no online checkout, just a direct conversation with Hermien to find dates that work for you.

We're close to the N1 highway and all major routes, SuperSport Park, the Gautrain station, and within easy reach of several golf courses and hospitals in the area — a convenient base whether you're here for business, sport, or just passing through.
